About

Compton gospel,
cinematic soul

Loren Oden is a Los Angeles singer, songwriter, and arranger with roots in Compton’s gospel tradition. His warm, expressive voice connects classic soul with the cinematic music of Adrian Younge, The Midnight Hour, and Jazz Is Dead. His debut solo album, My Heart, My Love, brings that sound into a personal collection of songs about romance, vulnerability, and connection.

Sixty-two recordings, and the voice is the thread through all of them.
