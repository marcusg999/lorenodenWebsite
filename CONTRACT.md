# CREATIVE CONTRACT — Loren Oden
Rolled 2026-10-08 · roll seed 1791422273033107305 · direction #2 of 7

## Direction (dice): LANTERN
Near-total darkness. The only light is a slow bloom that follows the visitor —
photographs, type and the catalog are revealed out of blackness like a spotlit
stage. Nothing is lit until you look at it.

## Three techniques (all three, woven in)
1. Higgsfield-rendered cinematic clip scrubbed as a scroll image-sequence
   (slow 3D orbit around a lit ribbon microphone in a black room).
2. Kinetic variable typography — the wordmark's width/weight axes breathe with
   scroll and pointer proximity, so the name inhales and exhales.
3. Living particle field — illuminated dust in the beam, flow-field physics,
   displaced by the pointer.

## Type
Hero / kinetic: **Anybody** variable (wdth 50–150, wght 100–900).
Editorial accent: **Instrument Serif** italic, for eyebrows and pull quotes.
Body: **Inter** — neutral, calm, 17–19px.

## Layout
Broken / asymmetric grid. 12 columns, content deliberately off-axis; photographs
break the margin; rules run at angles; no centred hero.

## Motion signature
3D orbit / fly-through hero driven by the scrubbed Higgsfield sequence.

## The one unmistakable moment
The lantern itself: the page opens essentially black, and the visitor's pointer
is the only light source. Moving it reveals the orbiting microphone, the dust,
and LOREN ODEN stretching open under the beam.

## Deliberately out of scope
No centred hero. No card grid. No light mode. No parallax-only motion. No Wix.
No CSS gradients standing in for photography.
